Help & FAQ

Find answers to common questions.

How do I calibrate KINE?

Go to Settings → Recalibrate. Sit up straight with your shoulders back and head level, then tap "Reset Baseline." KINE will record this as your ideal posture.

How long does the battery last?

KINE's battery lasts approximately 24 hours on a single charge. Charging takes about 1.5 hours using the included magnetic cable.

Can multiple people use one device?

Yes! You can create multiple profiles. Each profile stores its own posture baseline and data. Switch between users from the home screen.

Is KINE waterproof?

KINE is rated IPX4, meaning it's splash-resistant. It can handle sweat and light rain, but should not be submerged in water.

How do I contact support?

Email us at support@kine.io or use the in-app chat feature. We typically respond within 24 hours.
